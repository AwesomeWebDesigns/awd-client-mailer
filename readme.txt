Send simple, CASL-friendly emails to your clients from WordPress, delivered one by one through SMTP2GO.

== Description ==

AWD Client Mailer lets you email your clients about news, new services and openings without a marketing platform.

* Contacts with tags, CSV import and export
* Plain or branded emails, with personalization
* Test sends and a review step before every send
* Emails sent one at a time through SMTP2GO for better inbox placement
* CASL footer and one-click unsubscribe on every email
* Bounces and spam complaints update contacts automatically
* A consent and activity log for every contact

Looking to add more features? Contact AWD Digital at https://awddigital.ca/

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin zip in Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, then activate it.
2. Go to Client Mailer > Settings and add your SMTP2GO API key, sender details and business address.
3. Follow the deliverability checklist at the bottom of the Settings page.
